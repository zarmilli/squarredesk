Our Policy

01

All products are dermatologist tested and free from parabens, sulfates, and artificial fragrances. We are committed to clean beauty.

02

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and you will receive a tracking number via email.

03

We accept returns on unopened products within 30 days. If a product causes a reaction, contact us and we will arrange an exchange or refund.

04

We never test on animals. All formulations are certified cruelty-free and our packaging is made from recycled or recyclable materials.
